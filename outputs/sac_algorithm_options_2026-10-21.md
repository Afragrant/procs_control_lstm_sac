## Ranked proposals

1. **Small recurrent stability experiment: delayed actor updates.** Keep one critic update per existing learner call; actor and temperature every second critic update. On actor-update calls, complete the critic step before recomputing the actor loss with the updated critics. Keep target-critic Polyak updates on the existing per-critic cadence for this isolated experiment. This is a proposed SAC scheduling ablation inspired by TD3, not the original TD3 algorithm and not an established fix for this environment. Actor LR reduction previously failed; a change in update ratio is distinct but can also fail. TorchRL SACLoss.delay_actor means a separate target actor, NOT actor-update frequency.

2. **POMDP-oriented research proposal: history-conditioned privileged critic.** Keep deployment actor inputs unchanged. During training concatenate current privileged signals (train speed/target force, contact force, slow mean when retained; optionally additional mechanical state later) into the critic head alongside history features and candidate action. Use contemporaneous signals for Q_t and correctly aligned next signals for its target. Preserve history conditioning; do not assume a state-only critic is automatically sufficient under a history-dependent policy. These selected privileged signals are not the complete physical state. Train-only information must never enter actor observations or deployed normalization. Apply the same privilege to MLP when claiming recurrent-architecture gains.

3. **MLP algorithm benchmark: TQC.** Replace scalar Q regression/soft targets with quantile critics, quantile regression and truncated distributional targets while keeping the actor's 20-frame observation unchanged. This changes the learning algorithm, not merely network width. Intended to control overestimation; current logs do not show whether that is the dominant problem. Excessive truncation can make value learning too pessimistic. Budget and evaluate as a named published baseline, not a novel contribution or promised improvement.

4. **Optional MLP regularization study, lower priority:** critic LayerNorm as its own ablation; full DroQ only if explicitly budgeting dropout/regularization and its replay/update schedule. Adding LayerNorm alone is not a faithful DroQ implementation. Do not immediately combine high update-to-data ratios, dropout, PER and n-step targets.

## Validation

Keep observations, reward (including slow-mean choice), speed distribution, actor learning rates and evaluation cases fixed for each algorithm comparison. One change per experiment. Use 3 paired seeds for screening, independent test cases after validation selection, and report best/final scores, mean-force error, RMSE, contact loss and action statistics, plus wall time and actual optimizer-step counts. Existing STD/PSD selection does not directly constrain mean-force error. No new environment execution was authorized or started for this advice.
